features:
  - title: Any band, any filter
    text: Low and high cuts at 12 or 48 dB per octave, shelves, bells and notches, for every one of the eight bands.
  - title: Work on the graph
    text: Drag a dot sideways for its frequency and up or down for its gain, scroll for its width, double-click to switch it on or off, over the live spectrum of the output.
  - title: Scale to taste
    text: Scale turns every gain up or down at once, from half as strong to flipped, so you can set a curve a little too strong and pull it back.
  - title: No clicks
    text: Switching a band or its type crossfades it, and frequency, gain and Q glide, so automation and sweeps stay smooth.
  - title: Presets to start from
    text: Vocal Polish, Kick Punch, Bass Tighten, Mix Bus Air, Hum Notch, Telephone and Resonant Sweep.

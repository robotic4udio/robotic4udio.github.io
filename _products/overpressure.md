features:
  - title: A compressor first
    text: Threshold, a ratio from 1:1 to 20:1, attack from 0.1 to 100 ms and decay from 10 ms to a second. Input and output gain, with auto makeup when you want the level kept.
  - title: Saturation
    text: A saturation control after the compressor, from a little warmth to a thick, pressed sound.
  - title: Three stages of dirt
    text: Overdrive, Distortion and Fuzz, each with its own switch and amount, run in that order. Use one for colour or stack all three.
  - title: Clean where it counts
    text: The saturation and drive stages run at four times the sample rate to keep aliasing down.
  - title: Tame the top
    text: A low-pass filter from 200 Hz to 20 kHz after the drive takes the edge off whatever the dirt adds.
  - title: Watch it work
    text: A VU meter shows the gain reduction, with level meters on the input and the output.
  - title: Presets to start from
    text: Nine factory presets, from Gentle Glue and Vocal Leveler to Drum Smash, Bass Grit and Lo-Fi Fuzz.

Overpressure glues, levels and smashes, then lets you push the result through saturation, overdrive, distortion and fuzz. It works as a polite bus compressor and as the box that makes drums sound like they are about to burst.
